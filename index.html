What you could be missing out on

6 things most cleaning businesses could hand to automation

A starting list. On the free call we work out which of these would actually make a difference for you.

  1. 1

    Stop losing jobs while you're busy cleaning.

    Your competitors reply faster. They get the job.

    What we'd set up
    What's probably happening
    People ask three cleaners for a quote and usually go with the first good reply.
    What we'd set up
    Instant quote replies. The right questions. A ballpark price based on your rules. No more leads going cold while you're on the tools.
  2. 2

    Stop sending cleaners in blind.

    No keys. No access codes. No instructions. Just another headache.

    What we'd set up
    What's probably happening
    Keys, codes, pets and parking get sorted by text, one client at a time.
    What we'd set up
    Automatic booking confirmations, reminders and access details. Everyone knows exactly where to go and what to do.
  3. 3

    One sick call shouldn't ruin your morning.

    Stop scrambling. Start covering shifts.

    What we'd set up
    What's probably happening
    When someone calls in sick, finding cover and telling clients eats the morning.
    What we'd set up
    Available staff are contacted automatically until someone accepts. Affected clients are notified. You stay in control.
  4. 4

    Reviews and referrals

    More reviews and word-of-mouth, without asking face to face.

    What we'd set up
    What's probably happening
    Clients love the clean, but never leave a review.
    What we'd set up
    A thank-you message with your review link goes out after the clean, and regulars get a simple refer-a-friend message.
  5. 5

    Stop chasing money you've already earned.

    The job is done. Why isn't the money in your account?

    What we'd set up
    What's probably happening
    Small invoices are easy to forget and awkward to chase.
    What we'd set up
    Automatic invoices and overdue payment reminders. Less chasing. Faster payments.
  6. 6

    You don't need another person on payroll.

    You don't need a VA to answer the same messages, chase the same updates and repeat the same tasks every day.

    What we'd set up
    What's probably happening
    You're paying for help, but still chasing updates, checking tasks and picking up what was missed.
    What we'd set up
    Automate routine enquiries, quote follow-ups, booking reminders and job updates. No extra salary. No managing another person. No chasing someone to chase things for you. Let automation handle the busywork. Keep people for the work that matters.
