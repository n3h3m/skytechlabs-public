WHAT WE CAN BUILD AROUND YOU

Less chasing.
More things actually getting done.

Not a chatbot looking for a job. Practical systems built around the jobs you already need done.

01

Missed calls. After-hours messages. The same questions, again.

Catch the enquiry. While you’re on the job.

A missed-call text-back or AI receptionist can collect the service, suburb, property details and timing. Log the enquiry and flag the conversations that need you.

You stay in charge: You set the service area, opening rules and when a human steps in. An AI receptionist introduces itself as AI.

02

An evening of questions, calculations and proposals — for people who may never reply.

Quote without starting from a blank page.

Structured intake gathers the scope. AI drafts a quote from your approved price rules, or turns commercial walkthrough notes into a proposal. Agreed follow-ups pause when the customer replies or opts out.

You stay in charge: You approve prices, unusual jobs and final scope. Complex work still gets a site visit when needed.

03

A sick cleaner. A changed time. Five people who need an update.

A callout shouldn’t become a phone marathon.

Collect shift confirmations, offer cover to your approved backup team and flag gaps. Send approved schedule changes and keep client and cleaner confirmations together.

You stay in charge: You approve cover and roster decisions. Software can coordinate available people; it can’t create reliable staff.

04

Lost instructions, forgotten extras and “I thought you told them”.

One job record. Not six separate conversations.

Bring supported messages, scope changes and job notes into a shared record. Send checklists and reminders to the right person, and flag missing completion details.

You stay in charge: Your team controls access. Sensitive access information belongs in a secure job system, not an open group chat.

05

Unbilled work, forgotten timesheets and payment chasing on your day off.

Finish the clean. Start the invoice.

A completed job can trigger an invoice draft in your accounting system. Flag missing hours or extras and send reminders based on the payment status.

You stay in charge: You review exceptions and agree payment rules. Automation doesn’t guarantee payment or replace accounting advice.

06

Applicants go quiet. Happy clients drift away. Feedback reaches you too late.

Take care of the follow-through, too.

Gather applicant availability, send onboarding reminders, prompt post-clean check-ins and offer review requests consistently. Flag lapsed clients for a permission-based check-in.

You stay in charge: You make hiring and complaint decisions. No automated rejection, fake reviews or review filtering by sentiment.

Already have a VA or an office team? Give them a better system, not more copy-and-paste. We start with the tools you use — whether that’s a job system, shared inbox or accounting software. Integrations depend on each tool’s access and capabilities; we check that before recommending a build.

FAIR QUESTIONS

Before you
message us.

Sydney-based. Practical solutions.
Built around how you work.

Is this an ads or lead-generation service?

This page is about running the operation you already have: responding to enquiries, quoting, coordinating work and following through. It is not a promise to bring in new leads or run your marketing.

Do I need to replace all my software?

Not necessarily. We first check what your existing systems support. Sometimes a small connection or a better workflow is enough. If a tool can’t be integrated reliably, we explain the options before you commit.

Will AI quote or change a roster without asking me?

Only within rules you have explicitly agreed. We recommend human approval for pricing, unusual scope, staffing changes and sensitive customer issues. If the system is unsure, it should flag the issue rather than guess.

I already have a VA. Is this still relevant?

Yes. Repeated data entry, chasing confirmations and updating records can consume a VA’s time too. The aim is to help your people spend less time moving information and more time dealing with the things that need judgement.

How much does it cost, and how long does it take?

The initial strategy conversation is free. Setup, software costs and ongoing support depend on your tools and the scope. We discuss those before any paid work. We don’t quote a universal price or launch date for a workflow we haven’t assessed.

What happens after I fill in the form?

You choose WhatsApp or SMS. A draft opens with your business details and the issue you selected. You must press Send in that app. Once your message reaches us, we’ll get in touch. Opening a draft does not send an enquiry or confirm an appointment.

What about customer and staff information?

We work through what information is actually needed, who should have access and which providers will process it. Don’t send client access codes, health information, identity documents or payment details in this enquiry. Data handling and any messaging permissions are part of the setup conversation.

Is this only for big cleaning companies?

No. Established solo operators, growing teams and larger businesses have different needs. We’ll start with your situation. Other service businesses are welcome, and if you’re not trading yet, tell us so we can keep the conversation relevant.
